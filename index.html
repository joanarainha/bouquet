Good to know

How far ahead should I order?

Same-day orders are possible if you send them before noon. For big occasions, a couple of days ahead is best.

Where do you deliver?

Across central Porto for €4. Ask us on WhatsApp if you are a little further out.

What if a flower is out of season?

We swap it for a similar one in the same colours and tell you before we make it.

How do I pay?

On pickup or delivery, by card, cash or MB WAY.
